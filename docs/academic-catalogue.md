Conference locations have per-entry source URLs. Event editions are matched using DOI prefixes, never by conference name alone. IEEE spells the 2023 ICoSNIKOM location “Binjia”; displayed as the city Binjai. Location refers to the event venue, not proof of in-person author attendance. Missing locations remain omitted.

Google Scholar buttons search the exact title. They do not claim that every record has been independently confirmed indexed in Google Scholar. Do not add indexation, SINTA rank, quartile, citation counts, or unpublished manuscripts without record-specific evidence.
